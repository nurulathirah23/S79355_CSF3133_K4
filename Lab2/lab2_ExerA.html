About the Event

Career Day 2026 is an event that helps students explore different career opportunities and prepare for their future. Student also can learn about career paths, improve their job application and meet people from different industries

Career Talk

A guest speaker will share information about career opportunities, workplace skills and the experience of working in the industry.

Resume Writing Workshop

Students will learn how to prepare a clear and professional resume for internship or job applications.
